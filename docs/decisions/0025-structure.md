# 0025 — Structure as a top-level container of material components

Date: 2026-11-07. Status: accepted. Decided by: Ellad Tadmor.

## Context
Community feedback on minimal `material-component`: "The material property contains phase+description for describing the crystal structure, molecule, etc. and microstructure subproperty, but there doesn't seem to be anywhere for actual structures. How would one characterize things such as nano structures, free surfaces, thin films?"

`material-component` records what a substance is made of (`constituent`), its state of matter (`phases`), and free text (`description`, `microstructure`). Nothing records the form of the system modeled: a Si slab and a bulk Si supercell get the same metadata. examples/minimal.xml can mention surfaces only in `description`. `simulation-conditions › cell-periodicity` does not fill the gap: a slab is usually periodic in all three directions, with vacuum between its images.

Alternatives weighed and rejected:
- **A structure type inside `material-component`, alone.** The system may consist of several materials (a film on a substrate), and nothing would say how they combine.
- **A structure-level `type` holding the form of the whole** (the first form of this ruling). For a composite it has no consistent answer: a film on a substrate could be typed by the film (Thin film), CO on Pt by the substrate (Slab), a core–shell particle by both (Nanoparticle). The terms also mixed two vocabularies, forms of one piece of material (Slab, Nanoparticle) and ways of combining several (Heterostructure, Superlattice, 2D heterostructure); once each component has a form and a role, the second is redundant.
- **A typed stacking position and a typed crystallographic orientation per component.** Both serve layered structures mainly; an orientation between two components (e.g. (111)Pt ∥ (001)CeO₂) belongs to neither alone, and its notation would need its own definition. Both go in free text.
- **A recursive structure (parts containing parts).** The meta-schema, validator and pages assume a fixed tree; the structure's `description` carries any relation the roles cannot.

## Decision
1. **A new top-level property `structure`** takes the place of `material-component` in minimal (after `disclaimer`): required, repeatable, a container. Description: "A system modeled in the dataset, e.g. a bulk crystal or a thin film on a substrate, and the materials that make it up." Its subproperties, in order:
   - **`description`**: optional, not repeatable; string, `[]`, inapplicable, values null. Description: "An explanation of the structure as a whole, e.g. the stacking order of its layers, the number of periods of a superlattice, or how its components meet."
   - **`material-component`**: required, repeatable, moved from the top level. Description: "One part of the structure, made of a single material, with its composition, form, role and phase." Its subproperties, in order: `constituent` (unchanged), `form`, `role`, `fraction`, `phase`, `description`, `microstructure`.
2. **`form`** (new): required, not repeatable; string, `[]`, inapplicable. Description: "The geometric form of the component as modeled." Open terms (0013): 2D material, Bulk, Chain, Cluster, Molecule, Monolayer, Nanoparticle, Nanoribbon, Nanotube, Nanowire, Slab, Thin film. Note:

   > One of: "2D material" – A crystalline solid that is a single unit cell thick (typically a few nanometers or less), while extending infinitely or to large dimensions in the lateral plane, e.g. graphene. "Bulk" – A material extending without bound in all three directions, e.g. a periodic crystal or a liquid. "Chain" – A one-dimensional sequence of atoms or monomers, e.g. an isolated polymer chain or a monatomic chain. "Cluster" – A finite aggregate of atoms or molecules, smaller than a nanoparticle. "Molecule" – A single molecule. "Monolayer" – A single layer of atoms or molecules deposited on another material. "Nanoparticle" – A solid particle of nanometer size in all three directions. "Nanoribbon" – A strip of a 2D material of nanometer width. "Nanotube" – A hollow cylinder of nanometer diameter, e.g. a carbon nanotube. "Nanowire" – A solid wire of nanometer diameter, extending without bound along its axis. "Slab" – A truncated bulk structure that can be used, for example, to model surfaces. "Thin film" – A layer of finite thickness, free-standing or on a substrate. The form is that of the component as modeled, e.g. a substrate modeled as a slab is a Slab.

   Interface and Surface are not terms: each is a feature of a structure (e.g. of a slab), not a form. Heterostructure, Superlattice and 2D heterostructure are not terms: a structure of layers is described by components with the role Layer (a superlattice's periodicity, and a stack's order, in the structure's `description`).
3. **`role`** (new): optional, not repeatable; string, `[]`, inapplicable. Description: "The part the component plays in the structure." Open terms: Adsorbate, Core, Film, Inclusion, Layer, Matrix, Shell, Solute, Solvent, Substrate. Note:

   > One of: "Adsorbate" – A molecule, cluster or particle on the surface of a substrate. "Core" – The inner part of a particle, enclosed by a shell. "Film" – A layer of finite thickness on a substrate. "Inclusion" – A material enclosed in a matrix, e.g. a precipitate or an embedded nanotube. "Layer" – One of the layers of a layered structure, e.g. a heterostructure, a superlattice or a stack of 2D materials. "Matrix" – A material that encloses one or more inclusions. "Shell" – A layer enclosing a core. "Solute" – A material dissolved in a solvent. "Solvent" – A liquid in which a solute is dissolved. "Substrate" – A material on which a film, layer or adsorbate lies. Give a role for each component when the structure has more than one.
4. **`fraction`** (new): optional, not repeatable; float, `[]`, dimensionless, recommended unit null (0004 items 39, 48), minimum 0, maximum 1. Description: "The atomic fraction of the structure made up of this component, i.e. the number of atoms in the component divided by the number of atoms in the structure." Note: "The fractions of a structure's components sum to 1." An atomic fraction, as for `concentration` (0004 item 46): in an atomistic simulation it is exact, while a volume fraction depends on where the boundary between materials is drawn.
5. **`phases` is renamed `phase`**, becomes a repeatable scalar (string, `[]`) instead of a list (extent `[:]`), following the naming rule (a repeatable property stays singular), and becomes optional. Description: "A phase of the material." Terms: Amorphous, Single crystal, Polycrystal, Quasicrystal, Liquid, Gas, Plasma (the baseline's, with Crystal replaced in place by Single crystal and Polycrystal, and Molecule removed, since `form` Molecule records it). Note:

   > One of: "Amorphous" – A solid material without crystalline order. "Single crystal" – A solid material characterized by a repeating pattern of atoms that extends throughout it without interruption. "Polycrystal" – A solid material made of more than one crystalline grain of differing orientation. "Quasicrystal" – A crystal-like material with short-range order, but without long-range order. "Liquid" – A fluid structure (e.g. the molten state of a material). "Gas" – A state where molecules or atoms are far apart and only interact during collisions. "Plasma" – A gas comprised of ions and electrons. Omit for a component whose form is Molecule.

   An instance that gives Crystal or Molecule still conforms (the terms are open) and is reported as user-specified.
6. **`material-component › description`** keeps its description and gains a note: "e.g. a surface facet such as Pt(111), an orientation relationship such as (111)Pt ∥ (001)CeO₂, or the chirality of a nanotube." **`microstructure`** is unchanged.
7. **examples/minimal.xml** gets two `structure` entries for the Si GAP data, each one Si `material-component` with `<phase>Single crystal</phase>`: one of form Bulk (keeping the free-text description) and one of form Slab.

## Consequences
- Breaks 0.5.0 instances: `material-component` moves inside `structure` and requires `form`, and `phases` is renamed `phase`. A 0.5.0 instance's Crystal and Molecule are reported as user-specified.
- One pull request applies items 1–7 (schema, tests, regenerated page, example). minimal stays complete (`legacy_complete: true`).
- The rules "a role for each component when there is more than one", "fractions sum to 1" and "no phase for a molecule" are prose in notes; the validator does not check them.
- A co-continuous composite (neither material enclosing the other) has no fitting role term; an author may write one (open terms) until a dataset calls for a standard term.
